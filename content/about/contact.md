This page routes contact to the right channel for the right kind of matter.

## For spec bugs and proposed changes

Open an issue on the GitHub repository:

- **Spec bug or typo:** [open a spec-bug issue](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/issues/new?template=spec-bug.yml)
- **Proposed core claim type or change:** [open a core-claim-proposal issue](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/issues/new?template=core-claim-proposal.yml)
- **Other spec feedback:** [open a general issue](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/issues/new) on the repository

For substantive changes, the [LIP process](/spec/lips) is the formal mechanism. Issues are appropriate for smaller matters and initial discussion.

## For security vulnerabilities

Security disclosures go through private channels described in the [security policy](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Do not file security vulnerabilities as public GitHub issues.

Preferred path: [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/security/advisories/new). Alternatives are encrypted email to security@llmo.org with PGP, or plain email to the same address.

## For governance matters

The project author can be contacted directly at [spec@llmo.org](mailto:spec@llmo.org) for matters that cannot be discussed in public or that are not appropriate for the issue tracker. See the [governance page](/about/governance) for the full governance model.

## For discussion of proposed changes

[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/discussions) is the venue for discussion of LIPs and proposed changes during their review period. LIP frontmatter links to the specific discussion thread for each proposal.

## For everything else

Send general inquiries to [spec@llmo.org](mailto:spec@llmo.org).

## Response times

- **Security reports:** acknowledgment within 5 business days per the [security policy](https://github.com/openllmo/llmo.org/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **Other contact:** best effort, typically within a few business days. LLMO is maintained by a small team and is not a commercial product; response times are not SLA-bound outside the security policy.
