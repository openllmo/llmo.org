What

A canonical place for organizational truth.

Organizations have always needed a way to publicly declare what they are, what they offer, who runs them, and how to reach them. LLMO is an open standard for publishing that declaration in a machine-readable, cryptographically signed format at a well-known URL.

How

Sign it once, serve it anywhere.

An llmo.json file is a JWS-signed JSON document. The signing key is rotated through a key registry the spec defines. Verification is a single HTTPS GET plus signature check. No central authority, no per-vendor integration, no proprietary tooling.

Why

Built for AI consumption.

Search engines and AI systems increasingly answer questions on behalf of organizations. They need a verifiable source of truth that organizations themselves control. LLMO is that source. The protocol is open, the validator is free, and any organization can adopt it today.
