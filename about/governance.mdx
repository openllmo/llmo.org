---
title: Governance
description: Current governance model and planned transition.
---

LLMO is currently maintained by Nic Chavez as spec editor. The specification is expected to transition to neutral governance as adoption matures; details will be announced at that time.

Until the transition, changes to the specification, the claim type registry, and the accompanying schema are made by the editor. Proposals and feedback are welcomed through the channels listed on the [Contact](/about/contact) page.
